# The site serves corporate Visitors; parts and owner tasks hand off to parts.cat.com

Caterpillar already runs parts.cat.com for equipment owners, so this redesign targets Job Seekers and Curious Visitors only. The Cat AI Assistant borrows the parts.cat.com panel's look but not its jobs: serial number lookup, order status and parts search are out of scope and, if asked, the assistant links out to parts.cat.com instead of answering.
