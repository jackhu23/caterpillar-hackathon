# Caterpillar Corporate Site Redesign

A reimagined caterpillar.com homepage for people learning about Caterpillar as a company, with an AI assistant adapted from the Cat Parts store.

## Language

**Visitor**:
Someone arriving at caterpillar.com to learn about the company or find a job — not someone buying parts.
_Avoid_: Customer, owner, user

**Job Seeker**:
A Visitor exploring careers at Caterpillar.

**Curious Visitor**:
A Visitor wanting to understand what Caterpillar does, its industries, and its story.

**Cat AI Assistant**:
The conversational side panel that answers Visitor questions about the company and careers, modeled on the one at parts.cat.com.
_Avoid_: Chatbot, bot

**Suggested Prompt**:
A one-tap question chip shown in the Cat AI Assistant to start a conversation.

**Industry**:
A market Caterpillar serves (Construction, Mining, Energy, Transportation).

## Relationships

- A **Visitor** is either a **Job Seeker** or a **Curious Visitor** (or both)
- The **Cat AI Assistant** offers **Suggested Prompts** tailored to both kinds of **Visitor**

## Flagged ambiguities

- The parts.cat.com assistant serves equipment owners (serial numbers, order status); on this site those concerns are out of scope and hand off to parts.cat.com.
